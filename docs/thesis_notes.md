# Thesis evidence and limitations

- [Model evaluation](model_evaluation.md) has held-out category and severity metrics, natural English, synthetic-variant versus incident-level boundaries, vision, speech and forecasting.
- [Model provenance](model_provenance.md) documents sources and redistribution.
- [Annotation guidelines](annotation_guideline.md) specify how to obtain natural Banglish and real cross-language incident labels.
- [Security setup](security.md) documents API, administrator and data controls.

The strongest supported claim is a prototype trained on natural Bangla and derived English/Banglish text. An external natural English benchmark revealed substantial domain shift. Voice and duplicate matching quality remain limitations. Incident-level cross-language performance, natural Banglish and Bangladesh service time need independently collected test data.
